# Signals research and implementation decisions — 12 November 2026

This review uses primary research and official calculation/API documentation. It supplies candidate hypotheses and implementation constraints; it does not establish a profitable one-minute crypto strategy.

| Primary source | Finding used | CTS-C implementation / limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TradingView technical ratings](https://www.tradingview.com/support/solutions/43000614331-technical-ratings/) | A composite technical score groups moving-average and oscillator evidence across completed frames. | Separate source and category coordinators; never describe correlated variants as independent evidence. CTS-C is not TradingView's 26-indicator rating or an external recommendation feed. |
| [AQR: A century of trend-following evidence](https://www.aqr.com/insights/research/journal-article/a-century-of-evidence-on-trend-following-investing) | Trend evidence spans assets and much longer horizons. | Trend/momentum are testable categories. The cited monthly/long-horizon evidence is not proof for Micro/Low-Range or minute crypto fills. |
| [Liu and Tsyvinski: Risks and returns of cryptocurrency](https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877) | Crypto-specific momentum and attention factors warrant separate investigation. | Add source diversity without importing a claimed return, PF, sizing or intraday threshold from that paper. |
| [Gârleanu and Pedersen: Dynamic trading with transaction costs](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Dynamic-Trading-With-Predictable-Returns-and-Transactions-Costs) | Predictor strength, decay, correlation, risk and turnover interact with costs. | Include actual fees/size differences and all subsequent losses. Thin stops and large order counts alone cannot establish an edge. |
| [Bailey and López de Prado: Deflated Sharpe ratio](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) | Multiple trials and selection inflate apparent historical performance. | Freeze candidate selection before forward evaluation; retain failed families/policies and distinguish the previously examined interval from untouched evidence. No claim that CTS-C computes DSR. |
| [BingX official swap market streams](https://github.com/BingX-API/api-ai-skills/blob/main/skills/swap-ws-market/api-reference.md) | Compressed streams, heartbeat, actual trade time/IDs, and completed source data are separate concerns. | Record contiguous genuine 10-second bars and independently reconnect stream groups; do not interpolate ticks or backfill 10-second evidence from minute OHLC. |
| [BingX commission API](https://github.com/BingX-API/api-ai-skills/blob/main/skills/swap-account/api-reference.md) | Authenticated commission decimals such as 0.0005 mean 0.05%. | Convert once, preserve signed fill fees, distinguish fees from fill-price effects and exclude unobserved funding. |
| [BingX leverage API](https://github.com/BingX-API/api-ai-skills/blob/main/skills/swap-trade/api-reference.md) | GET reports side-specific account maxima; POST uses `side`, not the order's `positionSide`. | Confirm maximum leverage per owned direction before entry/add; never switch global position mode or change a foreign side. |

The source implementations are already present in the pinned CTS-A-O import described by `shared/cts-indications/source-manifest.json`. CTS-C now wires 206 short/medium source identities plus 225 category/ratio/minimum-source/input-mask coordinators into the same lane engine, Settings and accounting. The 431-identity state hash, all-source causal-prefix checks, source-conflict votes, independent source/timeframe/risk identities, Normal-disabled additional strategies and restart equivalence are regression tests.

Default Signals research sources are a fixed six-element starting set: EMA cross short, RSI reversal short, Donchian short, MACD cross medium, VWAP short and a Mixed 80–100% / minimum-three-source / both-variants coordinator. They were not selected from the new forward interval. The default optional three-point gate-window return-drawdown cap is a starting qualification control, not an empirically optimal loss limit. Source/coordination and risk settings remain editable and produce their own configuration identities.

The new public 50-symbol dataset has 72 hours of prehistory plus 24 chronological forward hours, ending 12 November 2026 at 03:07 CET (02:07 UTC). Available contracts and complete history determine admission, with no return screening. Part of the forward interval overlaps an already examined study; label it a regression/chronological comparison, not wholly untouched future validation. Native testing uses fresh observations and actual x02 fills where qualified entries occur. Until complete matched native samples exist, no live-vs-model minimum-distance calibration or profitable execution claim is established.
