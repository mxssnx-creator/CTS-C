## Independent accounting and native scope

The strict independent audit passes **1,900 ledgers / 32,303,530 closed-trade records**, including **22,860 exact Real/Live pairs**. It recomputes quantities, weighted fills, notional, gross/net returns, one modeled cost deduction, all post-Base admission proofs, every profile's totals and hourly close counts, and coverage denominators. Matching percentage returns alone are insufficient: fills, quantities, notional, cost and absolute PnL must also match their Real parent. Three mutation regressions reject double fees and a doubled Live budget even if summary totals and percentage returns agree.

The verified x02 fee pair is maker 0.02% / taker 0.05%. This closed-price replay models taker entry and exit fees plus slippage equal to 10% of their sum, using entry notional for both legs: 0.11% round trip. Exit-notional fee changes, native intrabar protection fills, minimum sizing, latency and funding are not modeled. Native accounting instead reconciles actual signed exchange commissions and excludes funding from the reported owned-fill net scope. BingX documents actual realized PnL and per-fill fee bases in its [PnL calculation reference](https://bingxservice.zendesk.com/hc/en-001/articles/11263299908623-Perpetual-Futures-PnL-Calculations-Explained) and [fee schedule](https://bingxservice.zendesk.com/hc/en-001/articles/11263240298255-Perpetual-Futures-Fee-Schedule). A passing selected window is not future evidence; see the authors' [backtest-overfitting paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf).

The isolated x02 lifecycle diagnostic passes owned long/short entry, exact 0.2-ratio add, resized protection, close and signed fee reconciliation. Two procedural closes net −0.019716 VST after 0.025516 VST fees; diagnostic PF is 0. Foreign inventory was preserved during that diagnostic and it left no residual diagnostic orders or positions. **Qualified native strategy execution PF is unavailable: zero closed strategy lots.** The paper observer warmed 50 symbols and consumed five further completed minute bars per symbol before a later ownership loss stopped it. This does not establish sustained native trading or profitable execution. Current durable control is stopped and exchange entries disabled. No x01 live verification is claimed.
