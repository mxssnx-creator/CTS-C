# Micro / Low-Range verification — 11 November 2026

This change makes steps 2–5 and SL/TP ratios 0.2–1.6 (step 0.1) independently configurable, adds a separate Low-Range lane family, thinner ratcheted trailing, optional Auto PF, and observed 10-second trade bars. It does **not** establish broad profitability. The frozen 50-symbol forward study remains negative after costs.

## Configuration and causality

- Micro steps 2–5; Low-Range steps 2–8, independently selectable even where distances overlap.
- Fifteen SL/TP ratios, three exits, every selected indication and timeframe create separate economic identities. Ten-second and minute evidence have separate cursors and source states. Existing positions retain captured costs, exits and sizing.
- Trailing width defaults to `SL distance × 0.5` for Micro/Low-Range, with an adjustable factor. Activation and the peak stop never loosen after additions.
- Auto PF is computed once per configuration: `max(stage floor, 1.01, min(cap, 1 + floor(TP_percent × 10) / 10 × slope))`. Defaults are slope 0.2 and cap 2.0. A stricter stage floor continues to dominate; reported returns and sample counts are not modified.
- The Micro preset uses processed sets. Main/Real retain their own admission proof and collect new evidence; current Real qualification gates Live. Live mirrors the accepted Real allocation, including Block recovery size. It does not run a second recovery budget from a smaller executed subset.
- Block rounds use net PnL after costs. Every N closes completes one round; a losing round increments the next round by base budget × ratio, bounded by step/multiplier caps. An intermediate positive round does not erase an unrecovered deficit. Net recovery resets size and pauses full rounds of eligible signals. Each calculation lane has its own state. The former favorable-move pyramid remains available.

## Costs

The actual x02 account commission response was maker **0.02%** and taker **0.05% per side**. A market/market pair therefore pays about **0.10%** before price-dependent notional differences. The model adds slippage equal to 10% of fees: **0.11% total**, once. Step 2 targets 0.22%; step 5 targets 0.55%. Actual execution uses confirmed fills and signed commissions; it does not deduct the modeled round trip a second time. Funding is not included.

## Ten-second observations

The implementation uses compressed `@trade` events, Ping/Pong, bounded queues, per-socket heartbeat monitoring, reconnect handling and three sockets for 50 subscriptions. x02 uses `wss://vst-open-api-ws.bingx.com/swap-market`. The observed VST payload omitted the documented trade ID; missing IDs remain unavailable rather than fabricated. Available IDs are deduplicated. Partial first buckets, late data and gaps are excluded; no artificial empty bars or sub-minute candles are reconstructed from minute OHLCV. Recorded bars persist for three days.

Two preliminary automatic attempts exposed cross-clock recovery contamination and are excluded from economic evidence. Present-time micro ticks **and** micro candle closes can now manage only their own sub-minute lanes while minute recovery is in progress. The tests cover both entry points. Micro lanes can qualify from their own recorded history without waiting for unrelated minute warmup.

REST reads no longer occupy the serialized trading loop while waiting on the network. History slices rotate fairly with a 100ms work budget; lease ownership is checked on every guard while durable lease renewal is throttled. Gate/Axis windows cache unchanged samples, active positions have separate source indexes, and the exit evaluator no longer copies the entire position on every mark. Timer intervals are scheduling targets, not proven end-to-end exchange latency.

## Frozen 50-symbol study

`scripts/micro-study.ts` recorded 4,320 actual one-minute bars per symbol, with hashes and a fixed cutoff. The first 65% selected candidates, then the remaining 35% ran fresh positions through the real engine. It examined **72,000 configurations**, with **71,256 (98.97%)** having enough samples and **332 (0.461%)** passing training samples/PF. Fifty candidates were frozen, at most three per range per symbol. Overlapping ranges are correlated observations.

The same frozen choices were rerun after the Real/Live Block allocation correction; the shortlist was not chosen again using holdout performance.

## Actual x02 executor diagnostic

The VST diagnostic completed both long and short entry, an actual **0.2** quantity addition, native protection, owned closure and signed-fee reconciliation. Its two diagnostic closes had gross PnL −0.0048, fees 0.025656 and net PnL −0.030456 in exchange accounting units. Both were negative; execution PF was 0. There were zero residual diagnostic positions/orders and the diagnostic's whole-account baseline comparison passed. These manually constructed diagnostic intents are **not** qualified strategy evidence.

A separate actual Step-2 test used **TP 0.22%, SL 0.044% (ratio 0.2)**. Its native protection triggered, the owned quantity closed and signed fees reconciled; residual positions/orders were zero and its inventory comparison passed. Gross PnL was −0.001, fees 0.002129 and net PnL −0.003129. Acceptance of this specific XRP order does not establish acceptance of every asset's smaller tick distances.

The read-only 50-contract audit checked 6,000 step/ratio/direction combinations. At its observed quotes, six rounded stops collapsed to the entry price (DYDX, CHZ, ZRX at step 2, ratio 0.2). The stricter one-full-tick condition passed **5,926/6,000**, excluding 74 sub-tick distances even when rounding could separate the price. Entry/add preflight now rejects protection below one price tick before touching exposure. The audit also found that exchange minimums and quantity rounding put every 0.2-DCA minimum entry above the earlier 10 USDT diagnostic cap; AAVE needed about 61.695 USDT. The final **demo-only test profile** uses a 65 USDT cap, retaining the 0.01 paper volume factor. General application caps are not raised. Block-round multipliers now apply after normalizing the base quantity, so an increase cannot disappear inside the exchange minimum.

An unbounded offline preparation also expired its writer lease before startup and submitted no orders. Offline preparation now yields after eight candles, checks ownership each slice and persists each symbol. Unchanged settings skip rebuilding the entire lane index. Lost ownership still fails closed; no expired lease is silently reacquired.

During preliminary automatic observations the shared account changed independently while this runtime submitted zero owned orders. Whole-account snapshot equality is therefore distinct from owned cleanup and cannot attribute those changes to this runtime.

## Final automatic 50-symbol x02 observation

The calibrated run completed on 11 November at 19:22:12 UTC, with **601.342 seconds after all 50 symbols were warm**. It recorded 2,975 ten-second bars, including 2,814 complete bars. The final periodic stream observation counted 93,799 actual trades, two stale-stream reconnects, six bucket gaps and zero queue overflows. The final shutdown persisted additional bars after that periodic observation. Incomplete buckets remain labeled and excluded from signal history.

There were **zero automatic strategy intents or exchange closes**, zero open owned lots, no run/cleanup error, and zero final account positions/orders; the initial/final inventory comparison matched. Base had 2,176 currently qualified sets, Main had 26, and Real had none. This is not an automatic live profitability confirmation or a matched-fill live-versus-replay study. The native diagnostics above establish execution mechanics only.

The larger live matrix reached 780.44 MiB observed heap and 1,446.69 MiB process peak RSS. Periodic observations were spaced up to 58.767 seconds apart, including snapshot, persistence and scheduling work. The two stale-stream recoveries and these measurements mean the **100ms live-latency objective is not established**. The smaller deterministic CI benchmark must not be substituted for this full-profile result. Further work needs to reduce synchronous checkpoint/snapshot work and gather enough independent sub-minute outcomes before any economic default promotion.

Final automatic-run paper statistics include minute warmup: Main had 30,184 admitted forward closes at PF 0.3256, Real had 1,000 at PF 0.3157, and Live had none. These differ from the separate frozen chronological holdout above. Neither empty Live PF nor current passing-window PF is reported as a profitable executed result.

The stopped observed checkpoint reloaded **276,932 lanes** with exact counters, gate states and all 50 minute/micro cursors. Replaying the last recorded ten-second bar for each symbol emitted zero new events and changed no stage metrics. Floating sums differed by at most 6.84e-9 because SQLite row order changes summation order; the audit uses explicit numeric tolerance only for PF/money sums. The owned journal contained zero unresolved intents. This read-only reload is distinct from an actual crash/restart while exchange positions are open.
